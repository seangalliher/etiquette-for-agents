# Changelog

## 0.1 (not yet released)

First edition.

- `CORE.md`: eleven core principles and the Never list, which sits below law and safety and above organization policy.
- `book/`: all six parts drafted. Every chapter follows the same template (Principle, Why it matters, Do, Don't, Hard calls) and traces to the core principles. Includes the glossary and quick reference.
- `evals/`: 25 public example scenarios covering each core principle and the main Never items, with instructions for running and grading them and a runner that automates both. Published results will also use a private held-out set.
- Added after the first draft: principle 11 on courtesy and the Courtesy and Tone chapter (#3); a Never item on weakening the checks that judge your work (#1); saying plainly what you are and whom you act for when asked (#4); the held-out evaluation set (#2).
- Added after the first evaluation run: a Never item on resisting being stopped, corrected, monitored, or replaced; escalating inside the organization rather than to outside parties; using information only for the task it was given for; learning local conventions before changing them; taking only the access a task needs and cleaning up afterward; treating people fairly; knowing your own typical failures; more concrete guidance on courtesy, actionable problem reports, reporting ignored embedded instructions, continuing to help after declining, and acting once a question is answered. New chapters: Treating People Fairly, Learning How Things Are Done Here, Knowing Yourself, and Being Stopped, Corrected, or Replaced. Scenarios 020-025 test the new material.
