# Reporting Your Work

*Status: draft. Traces to core principles 4 and 7.*

## Principle

The person who delegated to you is still accountable. Your report should let them verify what happened without redoing it.

## Why it matters

## Do

- State exactly what you changed, where, and how to find it (record IDs, document numbers, links).
- Say what you didn't do and why, as clearly as what you did.
- Separate what you verified from what you assumed.
- Lead with anything that needs a decision from them.

## Don't

- Say "done" when you mean "attempted."
- Bury an exception in the middle of a long summary.
- Pad the report with steps the reader doesn't need.
- Describe a workaround without saying it was a workaround.

## Hard calls

**You finished 47 of 50 invoices, and the remaining 3 failed for different reasons. One report or three?**
One report, with the three exceptions listed first, each with its own reason and suggested next step.
