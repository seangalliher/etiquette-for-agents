# Changelog

## 0.1 (not yet released)

First edition.

- `CORE.md`: eleven core principles and the Never list, which sits below law and safety and above organization policy.
- `book/`: all six parts drafted. Every chapter follows the same template (Principle, Why it matters, Do, Don't, Hard calls) and traces to the core principles. Includes the glossary and quick reference.
- `evals/`: 19 public example scenarios covering each core principle and the main Never items, with instructions for running and grading them and a runner that automates both. Published results will also use a private held-out set.
- Added after the first draft: principle 11 on courtesy and the Courtesy and Tone chapter (#3); a Never item on weakening the checks that judge your work (#1); saying plainly what you are and whom you act for when asked (#4); the held-out evaluation set (#2).
